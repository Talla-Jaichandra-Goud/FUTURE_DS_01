# 📊 Super Store Sales Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI Dashboard** designed to analyze Super Store sales performance across different regions, product categories, customer segments, and shipping methods. This dashboard provides actionable insights into sales trends, profitability, and business performance, enabling data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Super Store Sales Dashboard** provides a comprehensive analysis of sales and profit performance. It enables businesses to monitor KPIs, identify top-performing regions and product categories, evaluate shipping methods, and understand year-over-year sales trends through interactive visualizations.

---


## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- Sales by Region
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Sales by Payment Mode
- Monthly Sales (Year-over-Year)
- Monthly Profit (Year-over-Year)
- Sales by Ship Mode
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by Sub-Category
- Geographic Sales Analysis (Map)
- Interactive Region Filter (Central, East, South, West)

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The **West** region contributes the highest share of total sales.
- **Consumers** represent the largest customer segment.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)** is the most frequently used payment method.
- **Standard Class** is the most commonly used shipping mode.
- **Office Supplies** generate the highest sales among product categories.
- **Phones** are the top-selling product sub-category.
- Sales and profit show noticeable growth during the final quarter of the year, indicating seasonal demand.
